About me

In short

I think about what the future will be like and what my priorities should be. I was born in California, grew up in the great state of Massachusetts, and now live in Berkeley. I look roughly like this. I believe it is rational to manage the news, and I aspire to love my neighbor as myself.

Now

Before

I was previously a policy researcher at the AI Futures Project. I graduated from Williams College in 2026 with a BA in physics (magna cum laude). Before that, I was a 2025 summer research fellow at GovAI in London. Before that, I spent the 2024-25 academic year as a visiting student at Exeter College in Oxford. The previous summer, I interned at the Center on Long-Term Risk, investigating how language models reason about decision theory. Before college, I was a student at the Commonwealth School in Boston.
